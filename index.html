Privacy

Your information and choices

Search-brief details are used to assess personalized job-search support and are stored in a restricted database only after the live submission succeeds. Website analytics help us understand traffic sources, content viewed, navigation, engagement and where people leave, so we can improve Sirat Search and JibikaLinks. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising retargeting. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies when enabled; your analytics choice is respected.
